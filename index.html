About

Applied AI research focused on LLM reasoning, knowledge graphs, and explainable machine learning, with additional work in multimodal AI and time series analysis.

I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at Leading University, Sylhet, Bangladesh, where I work as an applied AI researcher. My current research explores LLM reasoning, knowledge graph construction, and graph-based retrieval-augmented generation (Graph RAG), alongside vision and multimodal AI and explainable modeling for time series data. I also build efficient, data-driven models for multilingual and low-resource language processing.

I completed my MSc and BSc (Engg.) in Computer Science and Engineering at Shahjalal University of Science and Technology (SUST), Sylhet, both with distinction. I currently serve as Principal Investigator on more than ten concurrent research projects spanning multimodal AI, clinical screening tools, and explainable machine learning, and I teach Advanced Machine Learning, Artificial Intelligence, and Natural Language Processing at the graduate and undergraduate level.
